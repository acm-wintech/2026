CALL FOR POSTERS AND DEMOS

We are inviting researchers to present a poster or demo of their work at WiNTECH 2026. ACM WiNTECH continues its established tradition of hosting a demos and posters session. We welcome proposals for demonstrations of novel work in wireless network testbeds, measurement platforms and results, and innovative prototypes. The areas of interest overlap those listed in the workshop Call for Papers. Posters may describe work in progress, while the session will offer an excellent opportunity to receive feedback and discuss early research. We emphasize that the demos and posters session is not a forum to display commercial products.

Demos and poster submissions should consist of a one-page abstract in the same format as regular papers, describing the demonstration plan or the outline of the poster content. Each submission must be a single PDF file no longer than one (1) page in length (including figures, tables, and references), using two-column format, 10-point size or greater. Submissions must include the author name and affiliation. The title of these papers must bear a "Poster:" or "Demo:" prefix.

All submissions should be made via the submission portal: {{ site.data.conference.submission_portal }}
