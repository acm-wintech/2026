CALL FOR POSTERS AND DEMOS

We are inviting researchers to present a poster or demo of their work at WiNTECH 2026. This is an excellent opportunity to share work in progress, receive feedback from the community, and engage with fellow researchers working on experimental wireless networking.

Demos and poster submissions should consist of a one-page abstract in the same format as regular WiNTECH papers, describing the demonstration plan or the outline of the poster content.

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

  • The title must bear a "Poster:" or "Demo:" prefix.
  • Page limit: 1 page including references, with 10-point font.
  • Use the same ACM format as regular papers. Templates are available here. In LaTeX, use \documentclass[sigconf, 10pt]{acmart}.
  • Submit via the submission portal: {{ site.data.conference.submission_portal }}.
